Short course · GeMAIHc 2027

Generative and Multimodal AI for Healthcare

Two 1.5-hour sessions on applying generative and multimodal AI in public and population health. Presenter: David Buckeridge, McGill University & Mila – Quebec AI Institute.

Session 1 of 2

Generative AI for Public and Population Health

LLM foundations, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, and a BEACON outbreak-surveillance case study.

Session 2 of 2 Coming soon

Multimodal AI for Public and Population Health

Fusing time-series and text data at population scale, with PandemicLLM and case-count-plus-media-text case studies.
